Rail Rush 3D assets (all CC0 1.0, Kenney - www.kenney.nl), converted to meshopt GLB with 256px WebP textures:
- assets3d/runner.glb: Mini Characters (character-male-b, with animations)
- assets3d/train.glb, rail.glb: Train Kit
- assets3d/blda/bldc/blde/bldg/bldj.glb: City Kit Commercial (low-detail buildings)
- assets3d/barrier.glb, lamp.glb: City Kit Roads
- assets3d/coin.glb: Platformer Kit
Engine: three.js r170 (MIT, vendor/), GLTFLoader + meshopt decoder (MIT, three.js examples).
