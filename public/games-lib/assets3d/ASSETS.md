## Hub car set (rc-*.glb), original work, CC0 (Gaming Hub)
- rc-sedan, rc-hatch, rc-suv, rc-van, rc-pickup, rc-boxtruck. Built procedurally in Blender 4.2 by `tools/blender/hub-cars.py` (extruded profiles, bevels, boolean wheel arches, tumblehome glasshouse, B-pillars, mirrors, lights, bumpers, chrome strip, 20-segment tyres with rims and hubs).
- About 2.2-2.4k triangles and about 40 KB each. Material slots are named paint, glass, chrome, rim, tire, trim, light_f, light_r and box; racer3x.js maps them to shared PBR materials (clearcoat paint, physical glass, env-mapped chrome).
- Used by every racer3x game (Lane Racer, Moto Lanes). The old cv-* names are aliased.

## Authentic Kenney Car Kit 3.1 import (2026-10-13)
- Author/source: Kenney, https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit ; archive mirror: https://github.com/Arslan12216775/kenney_car-kit/tree/master/Models/GLB%20format .
- The 10 imported `kc-*.glb` Git blob object IDs are pinned in `docs/audit/KENNEY-CAR-KIT-IMPORTED-2026-10-13.json`. Meshes embed their texture data; individual GLB source rights are CC0 as recorded by `LICENSE-Kenney-CC0.txt`.
- **Correction of previous provenance:** `cv-*.glb` are Divinux CC0 cartoon vehicles, NOT Kenney. `rc-*.glb` are procedurally generated by this project, NOT sourced professional models. Neither group is being relabeled as imported Kenney assets.
- Only one fixed player car is rendered by default. The remaining variants are in QA preview; unlock/equip verification and visual differentiation must be built and tested before public sale or equip. No model grants performance advantages.
