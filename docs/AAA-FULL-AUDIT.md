# Full Deep AAA Audit — Cosmetics, Achievements, Quests

## Objective
Every cosmetic, achievement, and quest must meet a true AAA visual and functional standard. No exceptions, no low-quality or blocky assets, no missing IDs, no ownership regressions.

## Audit scope

### Cosmetics
- All handmade banners, frames, titles, colors, effects, badges in `src/cosmetics.js` and `src/shop.js`
- All 12,000 procedural universe items generated by `src/shop-gen.js` + `src/cosmetic-universe.js`
- Shop rotation, pricing, Vault, earned gates, season track rewards
- Ownership persistence and equip flow

### Achievements
- Every achievement in `src/achievements.js` (per-game, overall, giveaway, community, leaderboard, secret, progress, social, legendary)
- Reward cosmetics must resolve to real designs
- Server-side evaluation only

### Quests
- 48-quest season pass chain (`src/pass-quests.js`)
- Rotating daily/weekly/seasonal quests (`src/rotating-quests.js`)
- Progress tracking, claim flow, and rewards
